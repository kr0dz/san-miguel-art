Fernando Castañeda.

Fernando Castañeda is a painter and muralist whose documented work on SMArt includes landscapes, architectural scenes, ornament, contemporary painting and large-scale interventions created directly inside lived spaces.

About the artist

Painting in dialogue with architecture.

In Fernando Castañeda’s work, the wall is more than a surface. Architecture, scale and light become part of the composition. Projects may incorporate landscape, architectural details, vegetation, ornament or contemporary forms depending on the space and the intention behind the commission.

His SMArt portfolio includes both finished work and process photographs, allowing viewers to see how a mural develops from initial lines and blocks of color into a completed intervention.

For clients looking for a mural in San Miguel de Allende, a commissioned painting or an artistic intervention for a residence, hotel, restaurant or other project, Fernando’s profile also provides a useful reference for scale, style and process.
