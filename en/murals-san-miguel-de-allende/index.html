Commission process

Artwork designed for that place.

Every project is different, but a mural commission generally moves from an initial conversation and site information to a visual direction, execution and completion.

Space & intention

Execution

Frequently asked

Commissioning a mural.

How much does a mural in San Miguel de Allende cost?

Pricing depends on size, surface, complexity, location, level of detail and execution time. Photos and approximate dimensions are useful for an initial estimate.

Can murals be created for private residences?

Yes. Projects can be developed for residences and private interiors as well as hotels, restaurants and hospitality spaces.

Can the artwork adapt to existing architecture?

Yes. Doors, arches, ceilings, furniture, lighting and proportions can all influence the composition.
