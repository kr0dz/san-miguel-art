SMArt · San Miguel de Allende Art

Curated art.
Not an endless catalog.

SMArt presents artists connected to San Miguel de Allende with more context: artwork, process, practice and a clear path for people who want to discover, acquire or commission work.

Our approach

A smaller platform can look more closely.

San Miguel de Allende has an enormous artistic identity. SMArt helps navigate it without turning it into an endless directory.

Each artist is added with a coherent visual selection and enough content to understand the work. Process, disciplines, related projects and inquiry paths are all part of the presentation.

What we do

Discover, contextualize and connect.

SMArt combines editorial presentation with search-friendly pages built around real artistic practices and local intent.
