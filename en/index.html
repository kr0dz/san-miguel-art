Art in San Miguel de Allende.

Can I commission a mural in San Miguel de Allende?

Yes. Private mural and painting projects can be discussed for residences, hotels, restaurants and other spaces. Photos and approximate dimensions are useful for an initial conversation.

Is SMArt a physical gallery?

SMArt is a curated art platform rather than a physical gallery. It is designed to present artists and connect interested clients or collectors with artwork and commission opportunities.
